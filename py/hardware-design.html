Two scanners.
One secure gate.

A compact entry box captures Kenyan national ID, school ID, or passport images and has an integrated fingerprint reader. A second, separate fingerprint reader handles checkout.

Main enclosure: 150 × 150 × 150 mm Passport support folds out to 180 mm wide GETPASS reader API
!
Size constraint: the main shell stays about 150 mm on each side. An open passport is wider than a 150 mm square, so the top tray folds/slides out to provide about 180 mm of support width and 135 mm of usable capture depth. Check the tray and camera field of view against actual passports and IDs used at each site.
Hardware layout

Entry capture + separate checkout

Keep document capture and fingerprint matching local to the gate. The cloud receives parsed fields and an opaque reader reference, not fingerprint images.

01 · Entry document box

Place the ID or passport under the camera on a glare-controlled platen. The fingerprint reader is a recessed, flush-mounted USB unit on the front slope.

Integrated entry reader

One reader for first-time enrollment and repeat-visitor check-in. Choose a model with a supported local SDK and liveness features.

CameraAutofocus 8MP+ document camera; diffuse LED; glare-safe glass.
ComputePi 5 prototype or compact x86 host; confirm reader SDK/OS first.
Passport trayFold-out support about 180 mm wide; ~135 mm usable capture depth.

02 · Checkout fingerprint reader

A separate USB reader sits on the gate desk. The operator selects an active visit; the visitor scans a finger and the local bridge reports a match for the linked opaque subject reference.

Standalone exit reader

USB to the trusted gate PC/bridge. No document scan at checkout. Keep matching and templates on the reader-side system.

ConnectionUSB to local reader bridge.
MatchLocal verification, then send only result + reference.
FallbackOperator review if the sensor or service is unavailable.

GETPASS integration

The existing reader API is the integration point; vendor-specific camera parsing, SDK calls, durable offline queueing, and device certification still need implementation.

Scan locallyOCR ID/passport fields; discard the temporary image after extraction.
Verify locallyReader performs fingerprint match and returns an opaque subject reference.
Bridge POSTHTTPS POST to /api/reader/verifications with a per-device token.
Central recordGETPASS validates, timestamps, encrypts visitor fields, and records the visit.
Gate confirmsPortal reads the pending verification result and displays the central response.

Target: show a confirmed entry only after the central API acknowledges it. During a network outage, label the event “pending sync”; do not promise zero delay or zero downtime.

Operational requirements

Reliability, privacy and validation

No zero-downtime promise

Use Ethernet plus 4G failover, UPS, health checks, and an encrypted durable outbox. GETPASS needs idempotent event replay before offline queueing can safely sync; neither network nor hardware can guarantee zero delay.

Protect biometrics

Keep matching/template storage local, encrypt device storage, use unique revocable reader tokens, and never send fingerprint images/templates to the server. Review lawful basis, retention and DPIA obligations before live school use.

Prove compatibility

Bench-test Kenyan ID and passport OCR, glare/blur, USB driver and SDK on the chosen OS, repeated check-in, checkout-to-active-visit matching, sync retries, power loss and recovery.
